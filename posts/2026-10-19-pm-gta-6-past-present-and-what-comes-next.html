GTA 6: A Decade in the Making, One Month From Launch + Nvidia

Monday, October 19, 2026 · Watch on YouTube

▶

Grand Theft Auto VI is the most searched topic in America today — exactly one month before it launches. This episode tells the full story in one straight line.

THE PAST. The series began in 1997 at DMA Design in Dundee, Scotland, as a top-down experiment on PC and PlayStation. GTA III took it 3D in 2001, Vice City followed in 2002, and San Andreas opened a whole state in 2004. Then came September 17, 2013: GTA V launched to roughly $800 million on day one and a billion dollars within three days — the fastest-selling entertainment property in history, with seven Guinness World Records. It has never really stopped: more than 230 million copies sold, and the franchise past 470 million.

THE PRESENT. GTA 6 stands in the state of Leonida — Vice City and five more regions — following Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval. Rockstar revealed it in December 2023, released a second trailer in May 2025, and dropped a surprise launch trailer on October 15, 2026. The game has been delayed twice and now launches November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version and no online mode have been announced, and the video says so plainly.

THE FUTURE — confirmed only. Take-Two reports earnings on November 9, preloads open November 12, and the game launches November 19. Everything else is labeled expectation, not news.

Today's American company

NVIDIA

Founded on April 5, 1993, in Sunnyvale, California, by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem — headquarters in Santa Clara since 1998. NVIDIA released the GeForce 256 in 1999, marketed as the world's first GPU, opened its chips to general computing with CUDA in 2006, and launched DLSS in February 2019 — AI upscaling that, by January 2025, ran in more than 540 games and apps. Company facts only: no prices, no predictions.
